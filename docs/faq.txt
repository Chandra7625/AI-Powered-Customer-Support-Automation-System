Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: Submit a support ticket through the customer portal.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days.

Q: Can I upgrade my subscription?
A: Yes, subscriptions can be upgraded at any time.

Q: What file types are supported for uploads?
A: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, and CSV files.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Submit a cancellation request through billing support.
