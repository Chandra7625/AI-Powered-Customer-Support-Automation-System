ABC Technologies Company Policy

Refund Policy

* Refund requests are reviewed by a human supervisor.
* Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days.
* Refund eligibility depends on subscription terms.

Subscription Cancellation Policy

* Customers may request cancellation at any time.
* Cancellation requests require supervisor approval.

Account Closure Policy

* Account closure requests must be verified before processing.
* All customer data will be deleted according to company regulations.

Support Policy

* Standard support is available Monday through Friday.
* Critical issues are prioritized for immediate review.

Escalation Policy

* Requests to escalate an issue to management require supervisor approval.
